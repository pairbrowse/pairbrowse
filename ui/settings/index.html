Agents and approvals

Always asks you first

Agents work in the browser without asking, except for these. They can't be switched off.

  • Final clicksA click that pays, deletes, publishes, sends or submits.
  • Confirm dialogsOK on a page's confirm after such a click.
  • PublishingBlocked until a passing pre-submit review in the last 30 minutes, then asked.
  • UploadsAny file outside PairBrowse's uploads folder, or not a plain document or picture.
  • Local addressesYour router, NAS and other addresses on your own network.

You answer in the agent's app, and in the PairBrowse browser on an approval card as well. The first answer counts.

Stopping agents

Pause stops every agent at once; Take control stops one. Both are on the agent panel.
